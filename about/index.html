about

Izumi has wavy, carefully and fashionably tousled silver hair.1 His eyes are light blue, and he has a fair complexion. Although he is a model, Izumi is described as being ‘pretty’ rather than conventionally handsome — having beautifully defined features and valuing a tidy, androgynous look. His face is among the younger-looking of the third years.2

(🦐)

(🎶)

In the novel, Izumi is often given cat-like descriptors, such as having the eyes of “a high-class, pedigree cat” with a cold gaze, his soft and fine “nekokke”[8] (cat fur-type) hair the same color as reflected moonlight[9], and being a “foul cat that leaves festering claw marks” on others.[10]

Izumi is known for saying “soo~ annoying” as a catchphrase, most notably when inconvenienced or embarrassed. A total of 57 times had been counted across 331 story episodes alone.[12]

He can be a harsh teacher and is prone to impatience, despite being quite thorough.[13][14]

Izumi excels at cooking and is strict with himself and those around him when it comes to fueling their bodies with proper meals.[15] He approaches his diet in a professional manner with a strong sense of responsibility.[16] To maintain his health and figure for his work as an idol and model, he almost exclusively prepares food for himself at home, focusing on nutrient-dense whole foods within a calorie limit.[17] On top of paying attention to his macronutrients, he supplements his diet with vitamins and minerals, as he is watchful for an adequate micronutrient intake.[18][19]

He doesn’t think sweets should be avoided entirely, just that they should be eaten in moderation.[20]
He gets bored of eating the same thing and is often on the hunt for new recipes.[21]
Later, when he moves to Florence, Izumi asks others to mail him items like Japanese seasonings and dried foods that are difficult to find overseas.[22]

As a young child, he cried and threw a tantrum the first time staff tried to apply makeup on him for a photo shoot. Prior to this, Izumi had been shown around a special effects makeup studio, and after seeing people’s faces look like they were melting off while getting makeup applied, he was under the impression the same would happen to him.[23]

Izumi displays traits of a delicate constitution. He is shown to be greatly affected by weather, temperature, and even what food and drink he puts in his body. His mental and physical health particularly take a hit when he doesn’t sleep enough. However, he is very meticulous about maintaining his health and is not one to let this affect his professional working side.[24][25]

High humidity gives him headaches, makes it difficult for him to sleep, and causes his hair to frizz up.[26]
The scent of alcohol makes him feel sick.[27]
He doesn’t tolerate heat well and often complains when he is exposed to hot weather,[28][29] but he isn’t particularly fond of the cold either.[30]
He once got heartburn from trying to finish a large cookie.[31] Additionally, eating too many sweet things[32][33] and staying up late[34] cause his skin to break out, sweating makes him uncomfortable and irritated, and he has been known to lose his appetite when it’s too hot outside.[35]
In A Midsummer Night’s Dream, heat stroke combined with jet lag resulted in his unstable actions and behavior.
When half asleep as a kid, he didn’t like how his eyebrows looked and almost shaved them off, figuring he could just draw them on instead. He was able to return to his senses in time.[36]

Izumi is often nicknamed “mom” or “sister-in-law” by his close friends to poke fun of the exaggerated way he worries about others, his mother-like nagging being the most remarkable part of it.[37]
He did ballet as a kid.[38]

Though it started out as simply something his parents wanted him to do, even hating it at first as he was always sore, he ended up putting his all into practicing due to his competitive nature and, over time, learned to love it himself after experiencing praise and success. He cites the smiles and applause from his first recital as when he fell in love with performing on stage, and making an audience happy as one of the foremost reasons he continued dancing and is an idol today.[39] His favorite ballet move is the "fouetté."[40]

He has a motorcycle license and would use it to get to Yumenosaki during his school days. He sometimes offers others a ride.[41][34]

If the engine is of low displacement (below 400cc), it falls under the category of an ordinary motorcycle license that is attainable in Japan at the age of 16. Though it has yet to be specified when Izumi obtained his license, he was habitually riding one by his third year of high school. In the Test of Courage card, Izumi is pictured holding a key closely resembling that of the Yamaha brand.
His favorite place to take his motorcycle out is a hill with a beautiful view. He mentions that he often goes alone, and that there may not be much to see, but he can stargaze when the sky is clear.[2][42] In a later comment on the subject, Izumi says that Florence has plenty of nice places as well.[43]
In the autumn of ES!! Year 2, Ritsu jokes about stealing Izumi's motorcycle while in Italy, which implies that Izumi has acquired an international license by then.[44]

He can knit.[45] Examples of his work include:

A scarf for Makoto, which can be seen in his Star Festival card, and in Makoto's Winter Live card.
A dinosaur plush toy for Tsukasa,[46] which can be seen in Tsukasa's Star Festival card.
A hat with a pompom for Anzu.[47]

Izumi has claimed to not care about gender or anything “typical of boys” since he was a kid. He goes on to add that he knows this is a serious topic for Arashi, but for him, “beauty transcends everything.”[48]
Izumi is confident that his beauty surpasses that of any woman.[49]
He is bad with horror, such as scary stories and occult-related things, to the point where it can interfere with his ability to sleep. Leaving his imagination to fill in the blanks of the unknown is what scares him the most.

He tries to hide this from his juniors, such as Makoto and Yuta Aoi.[50] Though he claims to not believe in ghosts[51], he will take deliberate action to avoid a potential curse "just in case." Izumi would later attempt to raise his tolerance for scary stories in order to expand his job opportunities.[52]

Sora described his color as “A very kind color, Sora’s favorite,[53] and as “silver, in both character and color.”[48]
Upon seeing his video search history, Leo points out that Izumi frequently watches cute videos of kittens and puppies.[54]

In the past, when Leo was surprised over how much he seems to love animals, Izumi replied that he at least likes them "more than other people."[55] This seems to only apply to small, cute animals, as in Cheval Live, he is teased by Tsukasa for initially being wary of the horses. Izumi's refusal to touch a snake is shown in his Springtime Beneath the Heavens card.

In addition to Japanese, he can speak Italian, as well as the basics of French and English.[56]
He rides a cross-bike (クロスバイク) — a versatile choice for commutes and everyday use, with wider tires that make it fit for the road and occasional terrain. Izumi notes that while he took up cycling to maintain his health, it ended up turning into a hobby.[57]
