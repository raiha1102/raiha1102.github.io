"Midnight"

“Yuu-kun”

In the darkness, an unnervingly cold gaze shone.

It’s already past the time when everyone should have left school. Beyond the room, the lights have been switched off, leaving the hallway completely dark. Moonlight is peeking in from outside the windows in a faint trace.

Someone was standing in the gap between those windows, leaning against the corridor wall.

He was wearing the Yumenosaki Academy uniform. Judging by the color of his tie, he must be a third-year student. His eyes were like those of a high-class, pedigreed cat. His fine, wispy hair was the same color as the moonlight streaming in. His appearance was so frighteningly beautiful that for a moment, I thought he was a monster. He was eerie, mysterious, and even inhuman.

Rei-san also has a demonic air about him, but he was always polite and kind to us. However, there was something menacing about this enigmatic young man in the corridor, as if he may attack the instant I turned my head away.

I flinched and instinctively clung to Mao-kun, who was supporting me.

The unknown, beautiful young man completely ignored us and confidently strode into the room as if he owned the place, rudely pushing us aside with one hand as if we were in the way.

It was all so arrogant. I thought about saying something, but when he glared at me for just a split second, I shuddered as if my heart had been gripped by an eagle’s talons.

There was no emotion in his eyes whatsoever, just as if he were looking at a pebble by the roadside. He was wiping the palm of his hand that had touched us as he shoved us aside on a handkerchief looking thoroughly disgusted.
It was incredibly rude, but I didn’t even have the energy to get angry. I shrunk back and clung to Mao-kun. Mao-kun looked just as bewildered, so it seemed they didn’t know each other either.

So who is this person then…?

He said “Yuu-kun,” but… Yuu— does that mean Makoto Yuuki?

Sure enough, the mysterious young man was staring straight at Makoto and only Makoto. There’s something strangely feverish about it—a world of difference from the way he looks at the rest of us. He seemed in a trance, as if gazing at a precious gem, the most valuable treasure in the world.

Narrowing his cat-like eyes, he speaks to Makoto in a cloyingly sweet tone.

“It’s so mean to run away—that’s going to hurt my feelings, isn’t it?”

“Geh!? I… Izumi-san!”

Makoto-kun recoiled at an incredible speed to the very back of the room. Is ‘Izumi’ the name of this strange person? The tone in Makoto-kun’s voice conveyed nothing but fear and disgust.

They don’t look like friends. But they seem to know each other, at least by appearance, as if they’ve met before. I couldn’t figure out what kind of relationship this was, and I was left feeling completely confused.

With an emotionless face that gave nothing away, Izumi said in a menacing tone:

“What’s with that ‘Geh’? You’re reacting like you saw a ghost or something. Sooo~ annoying♪”

Contrary to his words, Izumi seemed pleased that he’d called him by name, grinning from ear to ear.

At that point, Mao finally regained his composure and gave a perfectly reasonable warning.

“What do you think you’re doing? This soundproof practice room is reserved for Trickstar, so no one but staff members are allowed in!”

“Hmm? I wasn’t talking to you, so could you just back off?”

Izumi frowned, clearly displeased.

“I mean, can’t you speak to me politely, junior? A cheeky kid like you won’t survive at Yumenosaki Academy, you know?”

With an air of danger about him, he spat out the words in a purely malicious tone.

“I wonder if you want to be stepped on and squashed, you little bug♪”

—

Izumi’s attitude was so outrageous that even Mao, who is generally friendly to everyone, frowned and whispered to Makoto.

“…Hey, Makoto. Who is this person? Do you know him?”

“…I told you earlier, I got involved with a ‘slightly troublesome person’.”

Completely ignoring both Mao and me, Izumi kept staring intently at Makoto with a strange—almost predatory—look in his eyes. Even though he wasn’t looking at me, I felt an indescribable chill run down my spine and shuddered. Amid the oppressive silence, Makoto bravely stepped in front of Izumi, as if to protect me.

“This is the ‘slightly troublesome person’ I was talking about. Izumi Sena, a member of Knights, one of the strongest units at Yumenosaki Academy.”

Knights. I’d heard that name before, too. I’d heard it was a powerful unit with a long history, on par with RYUSEITAI that Tetora-kun and Sengoku-kun belong to. I’d also heard they weren’t participating in “S1,” so I figured it had nothing to do with me and hadn’t looked into the details.

I never imagined they’d get involved like this.

“For some reason, he’s always been picking on me since way back, huh~?”

He looks thoroughly disgusted, and Makoto-kun has completely lost all color in his face.

“Hey, can you stop whispering secrets to each other? It’s sooo~ annoying!”

Maybe it’s just a habit, but he keeps repeating this phrase that leaves a strange aftertaste. The creepy senior, whose full name is apparently Izumi Sena, approaches Makoto-kun without any hesitation.

“It’s so cold, I was just trying to cheer on my cute junior, but you ran away, Yuu-kun. That’s so mean, it hurts~♪”

He leaned his face as close to Makoto-kun’s as possible, cornering him against the wall. As if it were an afterthought, Mao and I were pushed aside.

With his hand pressed against the wall, cutting off his escape route, Izumi spoke to Makoto-kun as if whispering words of love.

“I just wanted to give you some advice. I don’t know what you’re so excited about, but… Yuu-kun, you have no talent. Why don’t you stop wasting your time trying to be an idol?”

But there was no love in those words; they were simply gloomy, scathing—and ultimately cold.

Izumi: “Yuu-kun, your looks are your only selling point. Why don’t you ditch those dorky glasses along with your hopes and dreams, and come back to the world of gravure modeling?”

He pinched Makoto-kun’s glasses with his fingertips, as if they were filthy. Like a frog stared down by a snake, Makoto-kun was trembling with fear, unable to react despite the terrible things being said to him.

But, gravure? Did Makoto-kun used to be a gravure model?

It seems Izumi is closely connected to Makoto-kun’s past. His tone is incredibly friendly—even though Makoto is rejecting him with his whole being.

I wonder what kind of relationship they really have…?

I glanced at Mao-kun, who seemed to know the details—and saw that he was grinding his teeth and clenching his fists tightly.

He was angry. It was only natural, given that a dear friend was being berated in a way that felt like being stabbed with a knife. He looked like he was about to lunge at Izumi-san at any moment, so, scared, I grabbed Mao-kun’s shoulder. Don’t do that—violence is not the answer.

“I mean, you just quit without telling me… Everyone’s sad, and you’ve caused us a lot of trouble. Don’t you feel even a little bit sorry?”

As expected, without paying us any mind, Izumi-san continued speaking in a taunting tone.

“People who run away from the first sign of trouble never amount to anything. I’ve told you that over and over again, haven’t I?”

He placed a finger on his own lips before pressing it against Makoto-kun’s, who stood frozen in place, unable to move.

“Hey, come back. Your good looks are the only gift God gave you, Yuu-kun. Throwing that away to do something nobody wants you to do is just a waste of your talent and your life, don’t you think?”

Izumi-san snatched Makoto-kun’s glasses, practically tearing them off, and threw them to the floor with a look of disgust. A sound of destruction—he crushed them with ease.

I gasped. It was as if a bomb’s fuse had been lit right before my eyes. Just as we were about to face off in the final battle, a monster named Sena Izumi suddenly attacked, destroying everything in its path.

That’s the feeling I had. This person is staining the bright future of everyone in Trickstar with a dark stain.

Izumi-san is now pressing his cheek against Makoto-kun’s face. He’s nuzzling him, saying “There, there,” just like a small child would do with their favorite stuffed animal.

“A gravure model doesn’t need a heart. You don’t need hopes, dreams, or even friends… As long as you’re beautiful, people will want you—and you used to be beautiful, didn’t you, Yuu-kun?”

Then, unexpectedly, he pushed Makoto-kun away.

Makoto-kun’s back slammed into the wall. As he groaned in pain and slumped to the floor, Izumi-san looked down at him with haughty disdain. Perhaps out of frustration, her shoulders were trembling slightly.

“Now it’s unbearable to watch. I’m disappointed, I’m really let down.”

He turned his face away, his eyes filled with a terrifying hatred that seemed capable of killing a human being. But suddenly, he smiled as innocently as a baby.

“But it’s not too late. Stop playing around and come back to this world, okay?”

Almost as if he were pleading, Izumi-san said this in a sweet voice.

“Yuu-kun is like a ‘beautiful doll,’ he shines best when he’s on display. If a doll starts moving on its own, it’s just creepy, isn’t it?”

“…This isn’t some kind of game.”

After a brief silence, Makoto shook his head and, using the wall he was still touching for support, somehow managed to stand up. Perhaps because he wasn’t wearing his glasses, his eyes seemed to shine strangely as he glared at Izumi.

“I’m serious.”

Izumi-san frowned, clearly displeased, and Makoto-kun desperately tried to explain himself.

“That’s right. It’s only in this place that I feel truly alive. Trickstar is the first treasure I’ve found in my otherwise empty life. I absolutely don’t want to lose it.”

With tears welling up in his eyes, Makoto-kun pleaded with heartbreaking sincerity.

“I don’t want to live my life suppressing my true feelings anymore.”

“Hmm…?”

Izumi-san’s smile faded again, and his face became as unreadable as that of a wild animal.

I don’t know what he’s thinking. Makoto-kun’s cry from the depths of his soul was so profound—so painful that even I, who was just standing by listening, felt a tightness in my chest—it was a precious thing that truly resonated with me. Yet, did it fail to stir even the slightest ripple in Izumi-san’s heart?

He just spat out his words coldly.

“That’s fine, really. You’ll fail at everything, get discouraged, and come back to me anyway. It’s just a matter of sooner or later—I’m even warning you that there’s no point in taking a pointless detour.”
