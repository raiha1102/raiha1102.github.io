raihagumi 

Izumi and Leo have known each other since their first year in Yumenosaki Academy. Since their enrollment, the two have been through various renamings of Chess, the oldest unit in the academy, before they formed what we know today as Knights.
For more information, see Chess.

One of their earliest interactions is Leo coming across Izumi, who, having taken a break from modelling to pursue his idol aspirations, is practicing his singing alone. Leo laughed at him, saying Izumi has "such a pretty face, but his voice is so out of tune!", resulting in Izumi shouting in frustration and throwing a chair at him.[47]

Izumi, having initially rejected Leo's pursuit of friendship with him after this event, would eventually relent after Leo's persistence. During this time, they became close enough that outsiders remarked on how the two seemed to "breathe as one"[48] and were always sticking together as if they were a set.[49] Izumi would often visit Leo's house, and Ruka, Leo's little sister, became friendly with Izumi after getting over her initial shyness.[47]

Despite being opposites—Leo embodying inherent genius, and Izumi embodying an ordinary person[50] who succeeds through extraordinary effort—the two continued to be drawn to each other. Leo's tendency to be sharp-tongued contrasted against Izumi's strong personality caused them to bicker frequently. Despite this, the start of their time in Chess (later known as Othello and Backgammon in more tumultuous days) was a period of comfort and peace for both of them. Izumi, initially impressed by Leo's talents as a composer and musical writer, respected Leo in his own way—especially for putting up with someone grumpy like himself. In turn, although Leo griped about Izumi's naggy disposition, he appreciated that Izumi wanted to take care of him and valued the other's hard-working nature. Leo viewed Izumi as someone kind at heart, thinking that Izumi simply gets misunderstood as moody or unpleasant all the time due to his attitude.[51]

Leo often told Izumi that he loved him, called him and his words beautiful, and clung to him; however, this forwardness frequently had Izumi reacting with mild exasperation and disbelief, brushing Leo off. Though met with numerous moments of irritation, Izumi in turn loved Leo's songs and loved singing for him more than anything. Izumi expresses that Leo's music of "love, goodwill, and passion" made him feel at ease and that what was created for them alone brought him happiness.[47]

Spring of their second year, Leo breaks his arm, leading to a point of consternation between the two. Leo had hidden the truth behind the incident, of which wasn’t revealed until later by Madara wherein Izumi had mentioned Leo had been feeling more distant lately—then unaware of what Leo had been dealing with at the time.[48] Shortly after, Leo became their unit’s newly appointed leader; his first move being to restore the unit’s name of Chess.[52]

While a conversation with an unusually sombre Leo would concern Izumi, Leo laughed it off and apologized for making things tough for Izumi. Izumi responded how this wasn’t the case—in fact things are better than ever with how he’s been able to wipe the floor with anyone who pisses him off, causing Leo to swallow up his complicated feelings as to him, Izumi’s happiness is his priority. It is around this point when fans have started calling Leo "Ousama," or "King," as a nickname derived from the last vowel of Leo's name—to which Izumi views as a proud testament to Leo's acclaim.[53]

With Chess now split up due to the new unit and live battle system, Izumi and Leo formed a new unit together so they could perform in Dream Festivals and other lives. The two settle on Knights, as Chess was taken, much to Leo's disappointment.[51]

Later that spring, now having splintered off as the newly formed Knights, Leo and Izumi's genuine feelings of care and affection for one another enter murky waters as their intentions and values start to misalign. Fighting with former allies increased, as did Izumi's infatuation with winning and Leo's desire to provide the music that would carve Izumi’s pathway to glory. Leo became further obsessed with making Izumi happy and viewed Izumi as his muse, taking on the malice of others head-on and crushing others with his compositions—his "weapons"—for the sake of Izumi's happiness. Izumi, blind to what was brewing in Leo's head, continued to ride the rush of it all.

On the day of the Checkmate performance, the day Izumi notes Leo began to crack, Leo arrives late. He reveals that he asked those he once thought of as his friends to choose between him or his music; doubt had plagued him, causing him to question if anyone else truly loved him the way Izumi did. Notably, he never approached Izumi with this choice.[54] The resounding answer of “his music” shattered Leo's heart and led him to blame his disillusionment on Izumi. From that point on, Izumi would wear this burden of blame like a scar on his heart.[55][56] Leo's reality as he knew it was upended and his love for others, which had masked their malice, was cruelly betrayed. Mentally unstable, Leo spilled his feelings with a heavy speech filled with desperation, concluding with how the entire world could hate him and he'd still be happy as long as he has Izumi by his side.[57] This simultaneously intoxicating vow was unnerving to Izumi, who had seen himself as "plain and boring," unworthy of such devotion. While Izumi goes through a flurry of complicated thoughts and emotions, Arashi Narukami notes that Izumi looks like "a girl who just got proposed to by the one she admires".[58] Having deep down the wish to be acknowledged by the world, Izumi, run ragged and not in the right mind himself, thinks that perhaps it could happen with Leo on his side—at the cost of falling to everyone else's level and getting Leo to serve him with all that he has, wringing him of his genius. This would be reminisced upon later, wherein Izumi thinks to himself he might have made a decision that could have "shattered Leo beyond repair" if they continued on the way they were.[54]

Leo's mental state further deteriorated when the losses they started experiencing embittered Izumi, making him moodier and taking away the smiles that Leo relied on to compose their "weapons." The bickering of happier days turned into Izumi and Leo having full-blown arguments and cycles of blaming each other. Through it all, Izumi never left; he blamed himself for Leo tossing away his "kind, loving heart" for his sake,[59] viewing it as his responsibility to accompany him to the end.[60] In the future, Izumi would ponder if they could have been happy back then had he not thought to wish for more.[61]

Sometime during this period Leo had composed a song for him titled "A Little Sena Izumi," a play on the serenade titled "A Little Night Music" by Mozart, the composer Leo loudly proclaims to hate.[47]

In the process of attempting to inhabit the role of a villain to protect Izumi, Leo would eventually break down completely. Leo thought that by acting crueler than needed, hurting himself and everyone around them, Izumi could have a reason for the aggression and betrayal he was feeling without directing any of it inwards.[54][59] Entering a deep depression, Leo remained truant even when his suspension was lifted, instead shutting himself away in his room. During this period the following year, Izumi stopped by Leo's house to get his signature in order for Knights to participate in DDD, as Leo was still leader on paper. Seeing the state Leo was in drove in the finality of the situation. Having no intention of "stroking the wreckage of a broken dream" but also not wanting to pretend the time they spent together never happened, a time of making his first friend and true equal, Izumi vows to continue forward and duel on Leo's behalf. Izumi’s iPod had been filled with the songs Leo composed for him over the course of their friendship, of which he would listen to during Leo’s absence.[47]

Later that spring, after Leo argues with his father about his absence from school, he runs into Madara who ends up forcing him on a trip of self-discovery overseas. Meanwhile, in the midst of Leo's absence, Izumi grew more unstable.[62] He takes his guilt and frustration out on Makoto Yuuki, seeking gratification and affection as a desperate means to protect someone he cares about, which he feels he failed to do with Leo.[61] Nonetheless, Izumi continues to perform beautifully, protecting Knights, hoping that Leo will one day return. Izumi is frequently on the lookout for Leo during this time.[63][64]

Leo returned to Japan that summer after working as a composer under a pseudonym for an overseas company. Having no intention to resume being an idol at this point, he muses how Izumi is probably the one person he owes an explanation to the most.[65] Madara later affirms this, mentioning how Izumi was worried and blamed himself for everything. Ashamed due to the prior events, Leo talks about how he contemplated working abroad permanently to create songs that Izumi could sing without the other knowing, adding that it would be enough for someone like him, who had barely escaped death. Although, Madara counters that without even being told Izumi would undoubtedly know they are Leo's songs.[66]

After rumors of being seen around school again begin to spread, Leo would finally show up at Knights' door thanks to the combined efforts of Tsukasa Suou and Anzu.[67][68] Izumi is notably more energetic despite initially doubting Leo's return, as even the police had given up on the search.[69] Acknowledging that he wronged Izumi, Leo expressed that he is thankful he got crushed under the weight of everything before he could end up dragging Izumi down with him.[70]

Arashi teases Izumi for being in a good mood due to Leo returning, but Izumi would again deflect by saying he's simply excited because this means Knights will have new "weapons" to fight back against those who made fun of Knights for being "knights with no king."[71][72] Leo's ensuing tyrannical and outright antagonistic behavior pisses Izumi off, who observes that he doesn't remember Leo being this unpleasant of a person. Even though Leo's demeanor did begin to crack at some point, the person Izumi remembered was someone who was more serious about idols; passionate and shining. After the events of Judgement, where Leo returns in an official manner, Leo continues to disappear on and off again much to Izumi's frustration.[73]

Overall, at this time, the two appear to be caught in a tentative dance when it comes to personal matters. Unsure of how their current relationship stands, Izumi and Leo both carry unresolved tensions and guilt, along with difficulty reconciling their past perceptions of each other with the newer version who stands before them today. Arashi brings up that Leo is probably exasperated by Izumi's display of reluctance to get involved,[74] as the latter has settled into an impartial stance that reflects a degree of emotional distance ever since Leo's return. During this time, Leo provided music for Knights routinely but would not yet diligently attend practice and even outright avoid performances, resulting in Izumi repeatedly hunting him down, not willing to let Leo act like he's not part of Knights—the place that has been protected all this time on his behalf.[75]

That autumn, Leo learns of the DDD incident that took place during the spring in his absence. Izumi's actions involving Makoto took him by surprise; to Leo, no one is stronger, yet even Izumi was hurt enough to fall to such drastic actions—Ritsu pointed out that even someone with high endurance like Izumi was breaking down and corroding, exhausted by holding down a battle with no known end goal, until Leo returned.[76] Although Leo hadn't wanted to meet Makoto because he felt he'd be siding with Izumi regardless, he ended up having an uncomfortably territorial confrontation with Makoto in which he told him not to worry anymore, as the "real" object of Izumi's affection is back, thanking him for keeping Izumi's heart from breaking, and that Izumi, while different in some ways, is still the person he loves.[77] In a later conversation, Leo gives Izumi his word that he will not run away, ready to pave the path to the end this time. Izumi welcomes him back from the heart, to which Leo responds that "he's home." [50] At the end of it all, Izumi acknowledges how he has been holding himself back, confessing that he's scared he's going to hurt Leo again, treating himself like a weapon instead of the protector he is should he get too close.[78]

Because Izumi had previously underestimated how deep Leo's damage was[79], he acts somewhat neurotically when it comes to Leo's wellness during the time they prepared for their Canary Hall live. After a disagreement that led to Leo disappearing from school once again, Izumi frets, thinking he hurt Leo like in the past. Izumi, honest after a long time, ends up speaking from the heart to Leo, assuring him that they all love him for who he is, not only for his music. Leo wonders when they became a unit that said "embarrassing" things like that.[80]

While Leo often would compare Izumi to a nagging mother, he enjoys being close to him to feel his warmth, which flusters and irritates Izumi, who isn't fond of heat. Leo, meanwhile, continues to express jealousy over Izumi's attachment to Makoto, but accepts it since he had been gone anyways. When giving Shu some critical insight during the Starlight Festival, Izumi says the other only superficially caressed the idealized surface of Nazuna and never genuinely got to know him as a person—Leo quips that he feels targeted by that comment, and Izumi cheekily answers that he is also speaking to "Leo-kun".[81]

As Leo and Izumi have been friends for a few years, they continue to rely on each other quite a bit. Even after Leo returned and their connection was strained, they grew closer again. As Leo states it, there's no way he wouldn't take Izumi's hand when he offers it to him.[82] To Izumi, Leo is one of those who has shown him that even people who "break" may shine again.[83] Although Izumi at this time would mostly call him "Ousama," he'd occasionally call him Leo-kun like he would in the past.

Upon their upcoming graduation from Yumenosaki Academy, many unsaid things come to a head as Leo and Izumi think about their paths for the future, and Leo throws the unit into chaos by proposing a new type of duel that would overthrow the current king: a Requiem.[84] Izumi, among the rest of Knights, was hurting Leo by walking on eggshells around him without realizing it,[85] and even wondered if Leo would truly want to quit being an idol—not being one to blame him if he got sick of everything due to the sadness he had experienced with it.[86] Elsewhere, Leo has a conversation with Makoto wherein he soundboards off him, saying that he is so intertwined with Izumi that the other's sins are as good as his own, and how it's painful to think about parting from him, but that he wants to get to know everyone from the ground up, including Izumi, the one he knew least.[87] After a heartfelt exchange between Leo and Izumi, Izumi would have the realization that he had protected him all this time, and they settle things into the simplest of matters: that if Leo is smiling, Izumi is smiling too.[88]

After graduating, Leo and Izumi move abroad in the early spring to pursue their respective passions, composing and modeling. Izumi lives in Florence, and Leo paid him almost daily visits. Leo initially rented a flat in Izumi's neighborhood and maintains that Izumi's place is more comfortable than his own allegedly shabby home. Though Leo didn't plan on staying there long, he decided to stick around after seeing that Izumi was having a rough time; he considers himself an example of how solitude weighs on you in the end, even if you're someone like Izumi who is good with being alone.[89] However, Leo makes up for it by utilizing his connections as a famous composer to aid Izumi with his modeling employment abroad, even though Izumi would later kick him out due to interfering with his goal of assessing what he can achieve on his own. Additionally, Izumi no longer calls Leo "Ousama," as Tsukasa is the new King of Knights.

After Leo's matter with GFK is resolved in the summer[90], Izumi is given an opportunity to clear the air regarding an assumption by Leo that he doesn't like being around him due to Izumi having kicked him out briefly. Izumi suggests that they live together as roommates from then on as a way to bring Leo stability, and since then, the two live together in a share house while they are in Florence. Izumi acts as a manager for Leo, accepting that Leo can also use his connections to help him without seeing it as a weakness.[91] While Leo expresses annoyance with how much Izumi nags him, he is happy living with him and thinks they can keep a good balance without repeating mistakes from the past.[92] Izumi compares living with Leo to taking care of a pet[93], notably making sure he is eating and even having gone as far as to throw Leo in the bathtub to keep up regular grooming habits, but in the end concedes that living together is overall a benefit.[94] As it is hard to erase the emotional wounds of the past entirely, guilt still creeps up from time to time, and the two bicker through rough patches as they juggle conflicting emotions regarding the past in context to the present. Leo doesn't truly blame Izumi for anything[95], and acknowledges that he is the one who willingly chose to hold him close, despite Izumi's apology for and assertion of being "too cold".[96]

Once the events of Knights VS Trickstar in the World Tour Arc wrap up in the autumn of ES!! Year 2, Izumi transfers his Florence residency to Leo's name before confirming that he is returning to Japan as his new home base of operations, thus ending this period of their cohabitation.[97]
