Childhood / Early Years

On top of being enrolled in ballet by his parents, Izumi was a relatively prolific child model with [[Makoto Yuuki]]. The two initially shared an agency, and though Makoto was far more popular[[PORTRAIT 2]], Izumi would take up the role of an older brother to the other boy, often guiding and teaching him as they navigated the industry together. In turn, Makoto would act as a source of strength for Izumi to push through anything that scared him, as he felt he had to live up to the other's admiration.

At some point early on, having been selected to model for a boys' cosmetics brand alongside Makoto, Izumi cried and threw a tantrum the first time staff tried to apply makeup on him. He had been shown around a special effects makeup studio prior, and after seeing people's faces look like they were melting off while getting makeup applied, he was under the impression the same would happen to him. However, his mother was able to draw his attention towards Makoto's expecting eyes, and even though he still rejected the makeup in the end, Izumi was able to pull himself together and wrap up the job perfectly. Considered a turning point for his career, Izumi often still gets recognized as 'that boy from the cosmetics ad' during his modeling jobs, and to this day Makoto's gaze from back then is what he pictures whenever he stands in front of a camera, intent on doing his best job.

This led to work piling up, and demands increasing in ways unreasonable for such small children that were also going through difficulties at home, and although he doesn't want it to seem like he is 'affirming the bad parts', Izumi considers everything, even the tough experiences[[Knights VS Trickstar|Knights VS Trickstar - Accordo 5]], important[[SS|SS - Effort 5]].

However, their time together at the agency was short, as Izumi would later be transferred out against his will by his parents after a disagreement with the agency management. The agency he transferred to was the one that [[Akiomi Kunugi|Kunugi]] and [[Arashi Narukami|Arashi]] worked at, and for a while he refused to get along with the two as a form of protest. Izumi occasionally still calls Akiomi "Kunugi-senpai" out of respect of him being his modeling senior.[[PORTRAIT]]

While not much is known about his period in ballet, his instructor is said to have lambasted him to 'dehumanizing' degrees. As it only served to bolster his resolve rather than weaken, Izumi came out on the other side valuing strong criticism.[[RELOADED|RELOADED - Chapter 5]]. What started out as simply something his parents wanted him to do, even hating it at first as he was always sore, he ended up putting his all into practicing due to his competitive nature and, over time, learned to love it himself after experiencing praise and success. He cites the smiles and applause from his first recital as when he fell in love with performing on stage, and making an audience happy as one of the foremost reasons he continued dancing and is an idol today.[[Soirée in the Canary Hall|Soirée in the Canary Hall - Epilogue 3]]

Yumenosaki Era

Reminiscence (Yumenosaki)
First Year

Izumi and Leo have known each other since their first year in Yumenosaki Academy. Since their enrollment, the two have been through various renamings of Chess, the oldest unit in the academy, before they formed what we know today as Knights.

One of their earliest interactions is Leo coming across Izumi, who, having taken a break from modelling to pursue his idol aspirations, is practicing his singing alone. Leo laughed at him, saying Izumi has "such a pretty face, but his voice is so out of tune!", resulting in Izumi shouting in frustration and throwing a chair at him.

Izumi, having initially rejected Leo's pursuit of friendship with him after this event, would eventually relent after Leo's persistence. During this time, they became close enough that outsiders remarked on how the two seemed to "breathe as one"[48] and were always sticking together as if they were a set.[49] Izumi would often visit Leo's house, and Ruka, Leo's little sister, became friendly with Izumi after getting over her initial shyness.[47]

Despite being opposites—Leo embodying inherent genius, and Izumi embodying an ordinary person[50] who succeeds through extraordinary effort—the two continued to be drawn to each other. Leo's tendency to be sharp-tongued contrasted against Izumi's strong personality caused them to bicker frequently.

Despite this, the start of their time in Chess (later known as Othello and Backgammon in more tumultuous days) was a period of comfort and peace for both of them. Izumi, initially impressed by Leo's talents as a composer and musical writer, respected Leo in his own way—especially for putting up with someone grumpy like himself. In turn, although Leo griped about Izumi's naggy disposition, he appreciated that Izumi wanted to take care of him and valued the other's hard-working nature. Leo viewed Izumi as someone kind at heart, thinking that Izumi simply gets misunderstood as moody or unpleasant all the time due to his attitude.[51]

Leo often told Izumi that he loved him, called him and his words beautiful, and clung to him; however, this forwardness frequently had Izumi reacting with mild exasperation and disbelief, brushing Leo off. Though met with numerous moments of irritation, Izumi in turn loved Leo's songs and loved singing for him more than anything. Izumi expresses that Leo's music of "love, goodwill, and passion" made him feel at ease and that what was created for them alone brought him happiness.[47]

Second Year (April-July)

April:
Spring of their second year, Leo breaks his arm, leading to a point of consternation between the two. Leo had hidden the truth behind the incident, of which wasn’t revealed until later by Madara wherein Izumi had mentioned Leo had been feeling more distant lately—then unaware of what Leo had been dealing with at the time.[48] Shortly after, Leo became their unit’s newly appointed leader; his first move being to restore the unit’s name of Chess.[52]

While a conversation with an unusually sombre Leo would concern Izumi, Leo laughed it off and apologized for making things tough for Izumi. Izumi responded how this wasn’t the case—in fact things are better than ever with how he’s been able to wipe the floor with anyone who pisses him off, causing Leo to swallow up his complicated feelings as to him, Izumi’s happiness is his priority. It is around this point when fans have started calling Leo "Ousama," or "King," as a nickname derived from the last vowel of Leo's name—to which Izumi views as a proud testament to Leo's acclaim.[53]

With Chess now split up due to the new unit and live battle system, Izumi and Leo formed a new unit together so they could perform in Dream Festivals and other lives. The two settle on Knights, as Chess was taken, much to Leo's disappointment.[51]

Later that spring, now having splintered off as the newly formed Knights, Leo and Izumi's genuine feelings of care and affection for one another enter murky waters as their intentions and values start to misalign. Fighting with former allies increased, as did Izumi's infatuation with winning and Leo's desire to provide the music that would carve Izumi’s pathway to glory. Leo became further obsessed with making Izumi happy and viewed Izumi as his muse, taking on the malice of others head-on and crushing others with his compositions—his "weapons"—for the sake of Izumi's happiness. Izumi, blind to what was brewing in Leo's head, continued to ride the rush of it all.

On the day of the Checkmate performance, the day Izumi notes Leo began to crack, Leo arrives late. He reveals that he asked those he once thought of as his friends to choose between him or his music; doubt had plagued him, causing him to question if anyone else truly loved him the way Izumi did. Notably, he never approached Izumi with this choice.[54] The resounding answer of “his music” shattered Leo's heart and led him to blame his disillusionment on Izumi. From that point on, Izumi would wear this burden of blame like a scar on his heart.[55][56] Leo's reality as he knew it was upended and his love for others, which had masked their malice, was cruelly betrayed. Mentally unstable, Leo spilled his feelings with a heavy speech filled with desperation, concluding with how the entire world could hate him and he'd still be happy as long as he has Izumi by his side.[57]

This simultaneously intoxicating vow was unnerving to Izumi, who had seen himself as "plain and boring," unworthy of such devotion. While Izumi goes through a flurry of complicated thoughts and emotions, Arashi Narukami notes that Izumi looks like "a girl who just got proposed to by the one she admires".[58] Having deep down the wish to be acknowledged by the world, Izumi, run ragged and not in the right mind himself, thinks that perhaps it could happen with Leo on his side—at the cost of falling to everyone else's level and getting Leo to serve him with all that he has, wringing him of his genius. This would be reminisced upon later, wherein Izumi thinks to himself he might have made a decision that could have "shattered Leo beyond repair" if they continued on the way they were.[54]

Leo's mental state further deteriorated when the losses they started experiencing embittered Izumi, making him moodier and taking away the smiles that Leo relied on to compose their "weapons." The bickering of happier days turned into Izumi and Leo having full-blown arguments and cycles of blaming each other. Through it all, Izumi never left; he blamed himself for Leo tossing away his "kind, loving heart" for his sake,[59] viewing it as his responsibility to accompany him to the end.[60] In the future, Izumi would ponder if they could have been happy back then had he not thought to wish for more.[61]

In the process of attempting to inhabit the role of a villain to protect Izumi, Leo would eventually break down completely. Leo thought that by acting crueler than needed, hurting himself and everyone around them, Izumi could have a reason for the aggression and betrayal he was feeling without directing any of it inwards.[54][59] Entering a deep depression, Leo remained truant even when his suspension was lifted, instead shutting himself away in his room.

Later that spring, after Leo argues with his father about his absence from school, he runs into Madara who ends up forcing him on a trip of self-discovery overseas. Meanwhile, in the midst of Leo's absence, Izumi grew more unstable.
